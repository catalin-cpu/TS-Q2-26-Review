1 · Profit rebuilt — the plan miss is all revenue

EBITDA $101.8k Q2 TD vs $118.6k plan; costs run $8.8k below plan (−$39.8k/mo vs Q1). MRR is the only gap.

2 · Conversion is up at every step — traffic is the problem

Up: the funnel converts better at every stage:

  • visitor→sign-up (G1) 1.6% → 2.1%
  • sign-up→trial (G1→G3) 68% → 86%
  • trial→paid (G3→G4) holding ~33%

Down: the real problem is traffic — visitors fell ~46k → ~29k (Jan→May), dragging G1 sign-ups −47% YoY in May (612 vs 1,153). A traffic problem, not a funnel one.

But: the visitors we do get convert better than ever — quality is holding. Volume is the bottleneck, not intent.

3 · Churn: net better, gross worse

Good: upgrades +18% and intensified churn-prevention (reactivations at a May year-high, partly seasonal) pushed net churn 0.84% → 0.57%; May closed churn-neutral.

Bad: gross churn is intensifying (1.59% → 1.90%/mo) — the real signal. Mostly small accounts leave (median $9), but 9 large churns ($109–396) drive 32% of lost MRR — a CS save motion, not a volume problem.

4 · The project priority tiering worked

A late start cost us some Tier 3 work — but Tier 1–2 shipped on time and the team executed excellently through the change.

5 · Q3 = Free Tier + Guidebook Redesign

Market-share bet (Free as acquisition engine) + retention bet (moment-based guest experience no STR competitor has). PRDs ready across the active roadmap; lower capacity (vacations).

4 Q3 Product Roadmap

Goal: product transformation — Free plan boosted by the new guidebook redesign; secondary focus on AI quick wins. Note: lower Q3 capacity (vacations). · Full roadmap sheet — PRDs ready.

🥇 Priority 1 — Free Tier Market share

Free is the acquisition engine first — every guidebook view a brand impression, every guest a potential host, the badge a referral flywheel. Limits, gates and prompts monetise underneath.
  • Limits & Feature Gates — 7 metered counters; 90% soft warning, 100% = email nudge via Intercom/Pipedrive (access not blocked), 130% = hard pause (restored on upgrade/reset); 7 new gates; upgrade prompts + comparison modal; admin/CS config + audit log.
  • Free Tier Experience — 4-tier pricing page; trial at Recommended → mandatory tier-choice → Free fallback with data preserved; tier-differentiated experience.
  • Referral Program & Banner — "Powered by Touch Stay" badge = referral banner ("Create your Touch Stay guidebook" + Try Now); always-on on Free, Tapfiliate opt-in for paid; new Refer a Friend menu.

🥈 Priority 2 — Guidebook Redesign Retention

From one-visit static wiki to a multi-visit, moment-based guest experience — no STR competitor ships this. PWA foundation for a future mobile app.
  • Guidebook Redesign — three modes: Home (timeline) / Info (wiki) / Assistant (chatbot + Contact Host); new orgs Timelines on, existing off with opt-out.
  • Timelines in Hub — topics + message templates mapped to stay moments; time triggers via PMS or invitation dates; shared Timelines library.
  • Guest Registration V2 — 3-block compliance-grade registration; Stay entity in CRM; PMS push / CSV / webhook.

🥉 Priority 3 — Geo Expansion: Spanish & French New MRR

English-only host journey is the hard blocker; i18n framework exists — this is configuration + content + per-locale SEO. TAM: France ~1.02M listings (#2 Airbnb market) + Spain ~320k ≈ ~750–800k host accounts; LATAM ≈ $5B upside. Spain-vs-LATAM decision before build (Alina PPC validation).
  • Website ES/FR + localized SEO · Hub UI with locale auto-detect · translated sign-up & onboarding · core KB translation.

4️⃣ Priority 4 — AI Strategy Upsell / Retention

Quick win completing the chatbot quality story; drives 2WM/SMS usage and gives larger clients an AI-generation reason to stay.
  • Message Hub — Train Chatbot Ready 4 Dev — interview-style training + document uploads (PDF/docx/images) with AI validation and pause/resume. Target Jun 30.

5️⃣ Priority 5 — Other Items Polish riding the redesign

  • Hub Overview Dashboard — new homepage: time-filtered metrics, check-ins, pending orders w/ Quick Approve, tasks, Common Questions (AI) with Fix Now, reviews, trial banner.
  • CRM Enhancements (rebuilt in the new Hub design system).

Monetisation levers active in Q3

  • Main focus: Free → Paid motion — limit-triggered prompts (90% warn, 100% email nudge, 130% hard pause), feature gates, badge removal, trial → tier-choice flow.
  • Paid tier simplification: 2 plans — Starter & Recommended — far easier benefit differentiation (PMS sync, Timeline triggers, contact collection, AI chatbot define the step-up).

Why Q3 dips before the Free-plan flywheel turns

  • G4 cannibalisation: with Free as the trial fallback, part of today's ~166 paid/mo parks on Free. New MRR takes the hit immediately.
  • Upgrade pressure lags: limits/gates bite only after usage accumulates — Free→Starter MRR arrives weeks-to-months post-launch.
  • Flywheel scales with base size: badge referrals grow with live Free guidebooks, which start near zero — compounding is Q4+, not Q3.
  • Landing on a soft funnel: sign-ups in YoY free fall (May −47% vs 2025).
  • Offsets: tiered ARPU +25%, trial churn converted into future pipeline, reactivations at year-high, Legacy Max protecting the base.

Our strategy deliberately trades short-term paid conversion for market share. The H2 plan should reflect this.

RECOMMENDATION — align the H2 MRR model with the Free-plan strategy:
  1. Remodel H2 at 1–1.5% monthly MRR growth (not ~2%) — the model needs a Q3 dip with later recovery, not a straight line.
  2. Why 1–1.5%, and how it firms up:
    • Funnel & churn improvements alone support ~2%/mo (if volumes don't decline further).
    • The Free plan adds a drag we can't quantify pre-launch (G4 cannibalisation, conversion lag) → we hold a 0.5–1pp buffer.
    • After launch, the audit events (Trial→Free, Free→Paid with time-on-Free, Referral Account Created) reveal the G4 dip, the Free→Paid rate & period and — toward end of Q3 — the flywheel.
    • The model is narrowed at the 90-day review.
  3. Hold the EBITDA line, not the MRR line — the cost base ($190.6k/mo) is the lever already working; commit to EBITDA targets through the dip so the Free bet isn't killed mid-curve.
  4. Score Q3 on leading indicators — Free sign-ups, Free→paid %, badge-referral G1s, time-to-first-limit — MRR as the lagging measure; judge the flywheel at the 90-day review.
